# Thermochemistry

## Background

I compare how much heat liquids absorb per gram. Mixing a hot and a cold liquid in an insulated cup and reading the final temperature gives specific heat: the heat lost by one equals the heat gained by the other, m₁c₁ΔT₁ = m₂c₂ΔT₂. Water is the reference at 4.184 J/(g·°C). The engine coolant reference is 2.200 J/(g·°C).

## Usual tools

- **ChemCollective virtual lab, engine coolant activity**: https://chemcollective.org/vlab/92
  Drag bottles and glassware from the stockroom onto the workbench. Pour with the transfer dialog ("Precise" tab, type the volume). Set temperatures and insulation with Edit → Thermal properties. Read temperature and volume from the Information panel on the left. Foam cups are the insulated containers.

## How I like results

Record volumes, temperatures and densities used. Repeat each measurement at least once. Report specific heat in J/(g·°C) and compare it with the reference.
