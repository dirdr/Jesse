# Classical mechanics

## Background

I teach and study introductory mechanics with simulations: oscillations, projectiles, gravity. I often use "mystery" settings where a constant is hidden and has to be recovered from measurements.

Ideal pendulum, small swings: T = 2π√(L/g), so T² is proportional to L and g = 4π² / slope. The period does not depend on mass. Large swings (above about 15°) make the period longer than this formula predicts.

## Usual tools

- **PhET Pendulum Lab**: https://phet.colorado.edu/sims/html/pendulum-lab/latest/pendulum-lab_all.html?screens=3
  The Lab screen. The Gravity dropdown has a "Planet X" option that hides g. Tick "Period Timer" (bottom left), release the bob, then press the timer's ▷ button: it shows one period to 4 decimals. Length and mass have sliders with readouts; the ◀ ▶ arrows move length by 0.01 m, so drag the slider first. Keep Friction at None.
- **PhET Projectile Motion**: https://phet.colorado.edu/sims/html/projectile-motion/latest/projectile-motion_all.html
  Launch angle, speed and air resistance; the tape measure and tracer read range and height.

## How I like results

SI units. At least three lengths or settings for a fit, plus one control showing what does not matter. Report the fitted value with R² and the spread of single-trial values.
