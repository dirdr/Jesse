# My lab

Scope: what H measures, what stays fixed, and the maximum number of trials.

## Prepare

1. Open the lab and confirm the right activity is loaded. A blank or wrong activity is a blocker.
2. Call `publish_update` with a short plan.

## Measure

3. Set the controlled conditions and read them back from the screen.
4. Run one trial and read the result.
5. Call `record_observation` immediately with the displayed values, units, raw text and evidence.

## Verify and report

6. Repeat once with fresh conditions if the protocol requires it.
7. Report observed conditions, results and remaining uncertainty.
